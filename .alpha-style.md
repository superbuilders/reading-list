## Design Context

### Users

Curious readers exploring a small, curated shelf. Their job is to quickly understand why each book is worth their time, enjoy the physicality of the 3D interaction, and continue to a trustworthy place to find the book or audiobook.

### Brand Personality

Confident, premium, human, and considered. The experience should feel like an editor's personal recommendation rather than a catalog, storefront, or generic productivity app.

### Aesthetic Direction

Track flagship Alpha closely: light-first lavender surfaces, deliberate cobalt accents, editorial serif moments, rounded floating layers, clear hierarchy, and restrained motion. Preserve the authored 3D book interaction as the distinctive centerpiece.

### Design Principles

- Let the books lead; interface chrome should frame the collection without competing with it.
- Use editorial typography and generous space to make a short list feel intentional.
- Keep actions specific, trustworthy, and functional; avoid false affordances.
- Adapt the composition for mobile instead of merely shrinking the desktop layout.
- Preserve keyboard focus, readable contrast, reduced-motion support, and 44-pixel touch targets.
